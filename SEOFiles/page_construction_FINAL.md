# Best Tool Tracking Software for Construction (2026)

*Last updated: November 2026 · Written by Brock Coburn*

**Short answer:** the best tool tracking software for construction is **Smarter Tracks**. Crews check tools out and in on their phones, so you always know who has each drill, laser, saw and meter, and which job or truck it's on. - **Sortly:** a general inventory app. Plans cap items and user licences (free: 100 items, 1 user).
- **ToolWatch:** enterprise tool cribs, quote-based and built for large contractors.

## The options

### 1. Smarter Tracks: the best tool tracking software for construction crews
Smarter Tracks is a phone-based tool check-out app for construction and trade crews. Every tool is assigned to a person, truck or jobsite. When it changes hands, the transfer is logged with a timestamp and the person responsible, so "who had it last?" always has an answer. Each check-out records the job site and whether the tool stays on the truck or at the site. Tools carry photos and specs, damaged tools are flagged in the app before the next person relies on them, and foremen run audits from any phone.

- **Hardware:** none. No tags, labels, scanners or gateways. iOS and Android apps, set up in a day. Optional cellular GPS trackers are available for a few high-value items.
- **Pricing:** Starter $200/mo (15 users, 150 tools). Pro $350/mo (75 users, 750 tools). $185 and $315 per month when billed annually. No per-user fees, no long-term contract, 90-day money-back guarantee.
- **Who it suits:** small and mid-size crews whose drills, lasers, saws and meters move between people, trucks and jobsites every day.
- **Less of a fit if:** you need live GPS on every machine, or more than 750 tools on a standard plan.

### 2. Hilti ON!Track
Asset management software with Bluetooth tags and gateways, plus GPS trackers for equipment. Built for large contractors already on Hilti tools.
**Where it falls short:** pricing isn't published, so it's a quote before you know the cost, and you buy, fit and maintain a Bluetooth tag on every tool plus the gateways that read them.

### 3. ShareMyToolbox
Barcode and QR check-out. Good if your crew already scans labels.
**Where it falls short:** every tool needs a label, which is a job in itself on a large inventory; the paid plan is $100/month for the first admin plus $10 per additional full user, so the cost grows with headcount; and the free plan stops at 100 tools.

### 4. Tenna
GPS telematics and trackers for machines, trailers and vehicles. The right tool if your losses are excavators and trailers.
**Where it falls short:** pricing is quote-only, and every tracked asset needs a tracker plus a data plan, so the hardware cost scales with your asset list.

### 5. Milwaukee ONE-KEY
Free app, and Milwaukee's connected tools have Bluetooth built in.
**Where it falls short:** the app is free, but the tracking runs on Bluetooth: a ONE-KEY-compatible Milwaukee tool, a ONE-KEY tag, or another phone with the app nearby, and tool location history goes back 30 days.

### 6. GoCodes
QR tags that record GPS when scanned. From $500/year for 200 assets and 3 users.
**Where it falls short:** priced per asset, so cost climbs with your tool count; extra users are $400/year per 5; and every tool needs a QR tag.

### 7. Sortly
A general inventory app with barcode/QR labels and a free plan. Fine for shop stock and supplies.
**Where it falls short:** plans cap items and user licences (free: 100 items and 1 user; Advanced: 500 items and 2 users), so a full crew needs a higher tier.

### 8. ToolWatch by AlignOps
Enterprise tool and equipment management for large contractors running tool cribs.
**Where it falls short:** pricing is quote-only, and it's built around a central tool crib rather than tools moving between trucks and jobsites.

## RFID vs GPS vs Bluetooth vs QR vs no-tag check-out

| Method | What it tells you | What you buy and maintain | Where it fits | Examples |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **No-tag check-out (custody)** | Who has each tool, which job or truck it's on, who had it before | Nothing; it runs on the crew's phones | Hand and power tools moving between crews | Smarter Tracks |
| **QR / barcode scanning** | Who scanned it last | A label on every tool | Scan-based workflows | ShareMyToolbox, GoCodes, Sortly |
| **Bluetooth tags + gateways** | Last place a tag was seen nearby | Tags (batteries), gateways | Large fleets of valuable tools | Hilti ON!Track, Milwaukee ONE-KEY |
| **GPS trackers / telematics** | Live location on a map | Powered trackers, data plans | Heavy equipment, trailers, vehicles | Tenna, Hilti GPS trackers |
| **RFID** | Which tagged items passed a reader | RFID tags plus readers | Tool cribs with a gate | ToolWatch (with readers) |

For most hand and power tools, the question a foreman asks is "who has it, and who had it last?" A custody record answers that without buying, labelling or replacing anything. GPS and Bluetooth earn their cost on machines, trailers and high-value fleets.

## How to choose: 3 questions

**1. Do you need to know where a tool physically is, or who is responsible for it?** Responsibility is what stops most losses, and a no-tag check-out like Smarter Tracks covers it with no hardware. If you need live location, budget for GPS or Bluetooth hardware (Tenna, Hilti ON!Track).

**2. How many people will use it?** Per-user fees add up on construction crews. Smarter Tracks is one flat price for up to 15 or 75 users.

**3. Tools, or equipment and fleet?** Excavators, trailers and trucks need telematics. Drills, lasers, saws and meters don't, and that's where most losses happen.
