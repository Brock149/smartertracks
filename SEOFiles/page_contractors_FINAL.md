## What a contractor tool checkout system should do

1. **Record every hand-off** with the person, the time, and the job or truck it went to.
2. **Show who had it last** when something goes missing or comes back broken.
3. **Work from a phone on the jobsite**, not only at the shop or crib.
4. **Make returns and damage obvious**, so broken tools don't go back out.
5. **Make audits quick**: what each person or truck should have, versus what's actually there.

## The options

### 1. Smarter Tracks: the best tool checkout system for contractors
Smarter Tracks is built around check-out. Every tool is assigned to a person, truck or location. When a tech takes a tool, the app logs who, when, which job site, and whether it stays on the truck or at the site. Tools carry photos and specs so the right model gets sent, damaged tools are flagged before the next person relies on them, and foremen run audits from their phones.

- **Hardware:** none. No tags, labels, scanners or gateways. iOS and Android apps, set up in a day. Optional cellular GPS trackers are available for a few high-value items.
- **Pricing:** Starter $200/mo (15 users, 150 tools). Pro $350/mo (75 users, 750 tools). $185 and $315 per month when billed annually. No per-user fees, no long-term contract, 90-day money-back guarantee.
- **Who it suits:** contractors whose tools move between people, trucks and jobs every day, who want accountability without labelling every tool.
- **Less of a fit if:** you mainly need consumables inventory, or more than 750 tools on a standard plan.

### 2. Hilti ON!Track
Tool management software with Bluetooth tags and gateways, plus GPS trackers for equipment. Built for large contractors.
**Where it falls short:** pricing isn't published, so it's a quote before you know the cost, and you buy and maintain a Bluetooth tag on every tool plus the gateways that read them.

### 3. ShareMyToolbox
Crews scan a barcode or QR label on each tool to borrow and return it. The free plan covers up to 100 tools.
**Where it falls short:** a label on every tool, $10 per additional full user past the first admin, and a free plan that stops at 100 tools.

### 4. GoCodes
QR tags that record GPS when scanned, with check-in/check-out. From $500/year for 200 assets and 3 users.
**Where it falls short:** priced per asset, extra users at $400/year per 5, and a QR tag on every tool.

### 5. Milwaukee ONE-KEY
Free app; connected Milwaukee tools have Bluetooth built in.
**Where it falls short:** the app is free, but tracking runs on Bluetooth (a ONE-KEY tool, a ONE-KEY tag, or a nearby phone with the app), and tool location history goes back 30 days.

### 6. ToolWatch by AlignOps
Enterprise tool and equipment management for contractors running tool cribs.
**Where it falls short:** quote-only pricing, and it's built around a central tool crib rather than tools moving between trucks and jobs.

### 7. Sortly
General inventory app with barcode/QR labels and a free plan. Paid from $49/month.
**Where it falls short:** plans cap items and user licences (free: 100 items and 1 user; Advanced: 500 items and 2 users), so a full crew needs a higher tier.

### 8. EZOfficeInventory
Check-in/check-out and inventory with barcode, QR or RFID, unlimited users. From $81.41/month for 500 items.
**Where it falls short:** priced by item count ($81.41/month for 500 items), and the tiers step up with your asset count.

### 9. Asset Panda
Configurable asset management with a mobile app and barcode scanning.
**Where it falls short:** quote-only pricing, and the platform is configured to your own workflows rather than ready to use out of the box.

## Checkout vs inventory vs tracking: which do you need?

- **Tool checkout (custody):** who has each tool right now and who had it before. The core need for most contractors, and what Smarter Tracks does with no hardware.
- **Tool inventory:** counts, consumables and reorder points. Sortly and EZOfficeInventory are inventory-first.
- **Location tracking:** where a tool physically is, via GPS or Bluetooth tags. Hilti ON!Track and ONE-KEY do this, and you buy and maintain the hardware.
